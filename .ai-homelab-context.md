# 7. NETBIRD DNS TROUBLESHOOTING

A packet capture was run:
```text
tcpdump -ni eth0 port 53
```

Observed traffic:
`192.168.20.97 -> 192.168.20.99:53`
with A, AAAA, Type64 and Type65 queries.

Normal domains successfully resolved, including examples such as:
- `www.google.com`
- `youtube.com`
- `m.youtube.com`
- `i.ytimg.com`
- `fonts.googleapis.com`
- `accounts.google.com`
- `firefox.settings.services.mozilla.com`
- `bag.itunes.apple.com`

Filtering behavior was also observed. Some domains returned:
- A `0.0.0.0`
- AAAA `::`

Examples:
- `iadsdk.apple.com`
- `ca.iadsdk.apple.com`
- `ads.mozilla.org`
- `googleads.g.doubleclick.net`

This established that DNS transport was working and that the zero-address results were filtering/blocking behavior rather than a resolver outage.

NetBird detailed status also reported:
`[192.168.20.99:53] for [.] is Available`

Troubleshooting principle: do not change a working DNS configuration just because Pi-hole returns `0.0.0.0`/`::`; interpret it as filtering when the surrounding evidence confirms it.
